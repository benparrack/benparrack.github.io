Studying Abroad

September 2026

Hello! My name is Ben Parrack, and I'm currently a junior studying computer science. My home school is Virginia Tech, but for this current Fall semester, im busy studying abroad in Stockholm, Sweden! Throughout college, my parents were always urging me to go abroad, especially my dad. He always told me the story of how he backpacked across Europe as soon as he finished college, and he had a such a good time that he wanted me to have a similiar experience. Most importantly, he always told me, You'll have all the time in the world to work, so go out and do everything when you have the chance. Or something similiar, but that's the gist of it. I really take this to heart because of how true it is, I really do have the rest of my life to work, and it's most likely what is going to take up the majority of the next 40 years of my life, if I'm lucky. As people, we really do need to take a step back sometimes and just take it all in, which is what I'm trying to do by spending my fall semester in Stockholm. It has been such an amazing opportunity to reconnect with nature, meet some amazing new people, and get a different perspective on so many different parts of life. I'm so grateful for my family which has put me in a position to be able to partake in amazing adventures such as this one, and I look forward to the future and hope I can continue making great memories. - Ben
