A bit about me

I'm a computer science student at Virginia Tech, majoring in Data-Centric Computing with minors in math and biomedical engineering. This semester I'm studying abroad in Stockholm with DIS, taking machine learning as my core course alongside electives in AI, data analytics and visualization, and app development. Over the summer I interned as an automation intern at GrayMatter Systems, wiring up PLCs and building inventory tools. I've also built a hydroponics control system, written a scraper to track gym occupancy so I'd stop guessing when it's busy, and dug into how well LLMs actually understand what's happening in a classroom. When I'm not doing any of that, I'm usually teaching middle schoolers how to program Arduinos through CS².
